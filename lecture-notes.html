Unit Economics

Know whether a transaction is worth pursuing. Track acquisition cost, order/project value, gross profit, fulfilment cost, project cost, contribution and repeat/referral value. Revenue alone is not enough.

Experiment Design

Test assumptions cheaply: state the hypothesis, run a small test, define the metric, record the result, then decide scale, fix, stop or test again. This protects a solopreneur's limited time and cash.

Decision Rights

Prevent the founder from becoming the bottleneck. For important decisions, define who recommends, provides input, approves, decides and executes. Output: a simple decision matrix for pricing, suppliers, projects, refunds, technology and partnerships.

How the Pieces Fit Together

Strategy chooses direction. Customer jobs identify demand. Capability mapping identifies what to build. The operating model makes it repeatable. Procurement and execution create transactions. Data creates learning. Experiments reduce uncertainty. KPIs create discipline. Case studies create trust. The strategy changes when evidence changes.
