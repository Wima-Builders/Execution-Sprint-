ENGINE 3

Manual RFQ

StepAction
1Receive requirement/BOQ.
2Clarify specification, quantity, location and deadline.
3Select 2–4 verified suppliers.
4Send identical RFQ.
5Compare price, stock, lead time and delivery.
6Present comparison.
7Coordinate customer decision and fulfilment.
8Record outcome and supplier performance.
Automation trigger: automate only after the manual workflow reveals the bottleneck.
