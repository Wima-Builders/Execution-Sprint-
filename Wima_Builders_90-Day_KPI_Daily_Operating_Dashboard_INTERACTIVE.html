1. How This Dashboard Works

Rule: the dashboard is not a reporting exercise. It is a decision system. Every metric must lead to an action, owner or management decision.
01

Capture

Record every meaningful enquiry, RFQ, quote, order, delivery and project milestone.

02

Review

Review the operating pulse daily, commercial performance weekly and strategic performance monthly.

03

Decide

Scale, fix, stop or investigate based on evidence.

15. The Wima Management Test

At every weekly meeting, ask:

1. What did the customer tell us?
2. What did the data tell us?
3. What did operations teach us?
4. Which supplier performed?
5. Where did money leak?
6. What should we repeat?
7. What should we stop?
8. What decision cannot wait until next week?

17. Final Operating Principle

Wima should not attempt to look like a large company. It should operate with the discipline of one.

The purpose of this dashboard is to create that discipline while Wima is still agile: one source of truth, clear ownership, measurable customer outcomes, rapid learning and evidence-based scaling.
